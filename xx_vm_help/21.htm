call dreg

It's assembly Pseudo-code .

It's means as the same of assemmbly instruction "push reg"

Operation

call dreg

Example

007065c8 call dreg call:76d15750 IsWow64Process arg_count=2 retn=00000001

"007065c8": It is a address of Pseudo-code

"call dreg":It is Pesudo-code

"call:76d15750":It is means "call 76d15750"

"IsWow64Process":the address of "76d15750" is named "IsWow64Process"

"arg_count=2":the count of parameters ,Parameters can be found from the previous stack.

"retn=00000001":the value of "call" returned
