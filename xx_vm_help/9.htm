push dconst

It's assembly Pseudo-code .

It's means as the same of assemmbly instruction "push const"

Operation

sub sp,4 ;mov dword ptr [sp],dconst

Example

006eda3c push dconst dconst=00000020

"006eda3c":It is a address of Pseudo-code

"push dconst":It is Pesudo-code

"dconst=00000020":it's a const,the size of "const" is dword,so the value is "00000020"
