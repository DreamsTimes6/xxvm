push wconst

It's assembly Pseudo-code .

It's means as the same of assemmbly instruction "push const"

Operation

sub sp,2 ;mov word ptr [sp],wconst

Example

00704f66 push wconst wconst=00000006

"00704f66":It is a address of Pseudo-code

"push wconst":It is Pesudo-code

"wconst=00000006":it's a const,the size of "const" is word,so the value is "0006"
